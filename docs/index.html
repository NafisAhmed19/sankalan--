সংকলন-ৎ · Sankalan-t

সংকলন-ৎ (Sankalan-t) is a warm, heritage-inspired personal diary, creative journal, and Memory Lane notebook app for Android (com.aistudio.sankalant.vknrzt).

Write daily reflections, design illustrated pages in the studio, organize multiple volumes on your bookshelf, and sign in with Google to personalize your reader profile and share product feedback.
