# Privacy Policy — সংকলন-ৎ (Sankalan-t)

**Effective Date:** November 20, 2026  
**Application:** সংকলন-ৎ / Sankalan-t (`com.aistudio.sankalant.vknrzt`)  
**Developer Contact:** nafisahmeddn@gmail.com  

## 1. Overview
**সংকলন-ৎ (Sankalan-t)** is a personal digital diary and journal Android application. We respect your privacy and collect only the minimum information required to provide account authentication and optional community feedback features.

## 2. Information We Collect & How It Is Used
### a) Google Sign-In Information (Firebase Authentication)
When you sign in with Google, we receive:
- **User ID (`uid`)** — used to identify your account securely.
- **Email Address** — used solely for authentication and displaying your signed-in account inside the app.
- **Display Name & Profile Photo URL (optional)** — used to personalize your profile inside the app and show your chosen author name on feedback you submit.

### b) Diaries, Pages, Drawings & Preferences
- Your journals, diary pages, sketches, stickers, photos, and app settings are stored **locally on your device** (using on-device Room database and DataStore preferences).
- We do not sell, rent, or share your personal diary entries with third parties.

### c) Cloud Profile & Community Feedback (Cloud Firestore)
- Your profile record (`/users/{uid}`) is strictly restricted so that only your authenticated account can read, update, or delete it.
- If you choose to submit feedback from Settings, your chosen display name, feedback category, rating, and message are stored in Cloud Firestore. You may edit or delete your own feedback entries at any time.

## 3. Google API Services User Data Policy
Sankalan-t's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the **Google API Services User Data Policy**, including the **Limited Use** requirements. We do not use Google account data for advertising or third-party data brokering.

## 4. Data Retention & Deletion
- You can sign out at any time from **Settings → Account**.
- You can export or clear local diary data from your device at any time, or delete feedback entries you submitted directly inside the app.
- For account or data deletion inquiries, contact **nafisahmeddn@gmail.com**.
